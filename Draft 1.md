## What Jordan & Prince Liked (keep)

- **"Step mantras"** element — praised explicitly twice (*"I like the step mantras. I like that."*).
- **Origin / brand-story narrative** has real potential — e.g. *"Two great minds came together and began a journey in search of luxury transport. To their astonishment, there was a gap in the market crying for luxury travel. From there, [Brand] was born."*
- Direction of travel overall — clearly better than previous rounds.

## What Was Rejected (remove / fix)

- **"House"** used to describe a vehicle — disliked by everyone. In a UK/Irish context it reads as a traveller mobile-home, **not** luxury. Believed to be an AI hallucination.
- **"Private chauffeur house"** — nonsensical / confusing.
- **"A house built on quiet change"** — clumsy phrasing.
- **"Household held to standard"** — off-brand.
- **"Fleet for every occasion"** — misrepresents a bespoke (often single-vehicle) service.
- **"Electrified" / heavy EV emphasis** — makes the brand read like an EV company that needs constant charging.
- **"Private information not disclosed… unless we're looking for investment on the stock market"** — scrap completely.
- Section title currently **too small**.

## Draft 1 — Requested Changes

1. **Remove "house" site-wide.** Only allowable exception: *"home on wheels"* in a cross-country context.
2. **"Private chauffeur house"** → *"private chauffeur — by introduction only."*
3. **"A house built on quiet change"** → *"change to your wishes — your wish is our command."*
4. **"Fleet for every occasion"** → **"a vehicle for every occasion."** ("Fleet" is OK only when describing the company-wide vehicle pool.)
5. **Reduce EV / "electrified" emphasis** → position EV as a *future aspiration*; stress vehicles **don't** need charging.
6. **Enlarge the section title** (*"title should be bigger"*) — stronger headline hierarchy.
7. **Scrap the stock-market / private-information disclaimer line** entirely.
8. **Add a fuller list of services** to flesh out the offering.
9. **Add security tiers** — e.g. **SIA-trained** ("SIS trained") personal protection.
10. **Swap** *"the future and beyond"* with *"tomorrow attended to"* per Jordan's notes (the swap was liked).
11. **Trial the off-white / cream palette here first** (Draft 1 is the colour testbed).
12. Insert the **Future-tab eco blurb**: *"committed to luxury travel that is also environmentally friendly… an electric option on the horizon."*
13. Insert the **political-risk blurb**: *"Due to political risks, our armoured vehicles are being designed and ordered for clients who want to feel that extra security."*
14. Amenities line: *"Fully stocked bar, finest spirits and champagne — specifics on request."*

## Branding Direction

Draft 1 is the **brand-story and vision** carrier — origin narrative, mission statement, and "the future" tab. Intended feeling: **established, aspirational, quietly premium** — a brand that feels like it has *always* served discerning clients. Warm the tone with **off-white / cream / gold**. Strip the clumsy literalism so the prestige reads as intentional.

## Strategic Notes

- The recurring **"house"** is a **terminology / AI-hallucination conflict**, not a creative decision. Prince jokes it might *"make a comeback,"* but it is confirmed **out for now** — the informal, non-luxury connotations undercut the brand.
- Jordan's guiding instinct: **aspirational aesthetics over literal reality** (*"whether it's a reality or not is irrelevant… it's about aesthetics"*) — but aesthetics must stay **brand-consistent** (no builder-type drivers, correct UK geography & right-hand drive).
- Draft 1 is positioned as the **testbed for the new off-white palette** before it's rolled into the merged site.

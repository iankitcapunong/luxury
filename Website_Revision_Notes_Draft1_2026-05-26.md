# Draft 1 Analysis

## What Was Liked
- **"Step mantras"** element — explicitly liked twice ("I like the step mantras. I like that.").
- Recognized as **far better than previous rounds** ("both drafts are better than the last drafts tremendously").
- Jordan **personally preferred Draft 1** overall (his assistant preferred Draft 2) — though he conceded his judgment was clouded by the wording.
- Strong **origin-story narrative potential** ("Two great minds came together and began a journey in search of luxury transport…").

## What Was Rejected
- Prince: **"I'm not enjoying the design all the way."**
- Repeated, **AI-hallucinated use of "house"** to describe a vehicle — strongly disliked by all.
- **"Private chauffeur house"** — confusing, rejected.
- **"A house built on quiet change"** — wording reworked.
- **"Fleet for every occasion"** — misrepresents a bespoke (often single-vehicle) service.
- **"Electrified" / electric over-emphasis** — makes the brand read like an EV company needing constant charging.
- **"Private information not disclosed… unless we're looking for investment on the stock market"** — scrap completely.

## Requested Changes
1. **Remove "house" entirely** from the whole website (one allowable exception: "home on wheels" only in a cross-country context).
2. Rewrite **"private chauffeur house"** → "private chauffeur" / "by introduction only."
3. Replace **"a house built on quiet change"** → reframe around **"your wish is our command"** / "change to your wishes."
4. Replace **"fleet for every occasion"** → **"a vehicle for every occasion."**
5. **Stop over-using "electrified"** — reposition EV as a *future direction*, not a present constraint.
6. **Enlarge the section title** ("title should be bigger").
7. **Scrap the "private information not disclosed" stock-market line** entirely.
8. **Add a fuller list of services** (fill out the offering).
9. **Add specific security tiers** — e.g., **SIA-trained** ("SIS trained") personal protection.
10. Imagery: **add London** scenes (Tower Bridge / Chelsea Bridge), day + night variants.
11. **Fix steering wheel** to the correct (UK right-hand) side.
12. **Remove "builder-type men"** from the driver's seat — off-brand demographic.
13. Trial the **off-white** palette on Draft 1.
14. Future tab copy → use Jordan's blurb: *"committed to providing luxury travel that is also environmentally friendly… an electric option on the horizon."*
15. "Fully stocked bar, finest spirits and champagne — **specifics on request.**"

## Branding Direction
Draft 1 should carry the **brand story and vision** (origin narrative, mission statement, the future) while shedding clumsy literalism. Intended feeling: **established, aspirational, quietly premium** — a brand that has "always" existed for discerning clients. Off-white/cream/gold to warm the luxury tone.

## Copywriting Issues
- "house" / "household held to standard" (awkward + off-brand)
- "private chauffeur house" (nonsensical)
- "a house built on quiet change" (clumsy)
- "fleet for every occasion" (factually wrong for bespoke service)
- "electrified" (wrong emphasis)
- "tomorrow attended to" (doesn't make sense as a swap for "the future and beyond")
- Generic AI filler instead of intentional brand copy

## Design Issues
- **Layout/hierarchy:** key section title too small.
- **Color:** pure white feels less luxurious than off-white/cream/gold.
- **Imagery:** wrong demographic (builders), wrong-side steering wheel, too desert-centric, missing London.
- **Typography:** needs more confident headline scale.
- **Tone alignment:** sections "all not aligned" — inconsistent voice.

## Strategic Notes
- The recurring **"house"** problem is treated as a **terminology/AI-hallucination conflict**, not a creative choice — Prince even jokes the word "lurks" and might "make a comeback," but it is confirmed out for now.
- Jordan's instinct: **literal accuracy matters less than aspirational aesthetics** ("whether it's a reality or not, that's irrelevant… it's about aesthetics") — but **brand-consistent** aesthetics (no builders, correct geography).

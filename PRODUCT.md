# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

High-net-worth individuals, executives, celebrities, and discerning private clients in London (Mayfair and beyond) who need chauffeured luxury transport. Often arriving by referral, not advertising. Their context: planning private travel, airport/airfield transfers, events, protection-aware journeys, or long-distance trips where discretion, reliability, and refinement matter more than price. They are evaluating whether this company is trustworthy and "their kind of thing" within seconds of landing.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for **Luxury Transport** (Draft 1, luxury-trans.vercel.app): a private chauffeur and luxury transport service. It exists to convey discretion, taste, and quiet competence, and to convert qualified interest into an enquiry. Success = a visitor feels this is a discreet, elite service "by introduction only" and submits an enquiry, without the site ever feeling like a mass-market car-hire booking flow.

## Brand Personality

Quiet luxury. Discreet, composed, understated, confident. Voice lines that define it: "Quietly Driven.", "Seen by few. Trusted by all.", "Private chauffeur · By introduction only." Three words: **discreet, refined, assured.** Emotional goal: calm reassurance and aspiration, never hype. The reader should feel they've found something private and well-kept, not been sold to.

## Anti-references

- Mass-market ride-hailing / car-hire booking UIs (Uber, Addison Lee aggressiveness, price-led CTAs).
- Flashy "Vegas limo" / stretch-Hummer party aesthetic: gold-chrome, neon, loud.
- Generic SaaS landing-page scaffolding (hero-metric template, identical icon-card grids, eyebrow on every section).
- Anything that reads as paparazzi-bait or celebrity-gossip adjacent (protection is framed around safety, not fame).

## Design Principles

1. **Discretion over display.** The design should whisper. Restraint is the luxury signal; nothing shouts for attention.
2. **Let imagery and motion carry mood.** Full-bleed video/photography and slow, exponential reveals do the emotional work; copy stays spare.
3. **Editorial composure.** Generous whitespace, deliberate pacing, one dominant idea per fold. Refinement reads as taste.
4. **Preserve the committed identity.** Cormorant Garamond + off-white/gold/black is the brand. Polish refines within it; it does not redesign it.
5. **Earn the enquiry.** Every page builds trust toward a single, calm conversion: the enquiry. No pressure, no clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Particular watch-items for this brand: body text is set in Cormorant Garamond (a thin serif) on warm off-white, so body legibility and contrast (≥4.5:1) need active guarding at small sizes. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (heavy reveal/ken-burns/video usage). Visible focus indicators, semantic landmarks, descriptive alt text written in the brand voice, and 44px touch targets on mobile.
